06 / QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOU MESSAGE US.

A few quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

01 How can I get help with my event ticket?

Use the event support route above and include your booking details or event information. Our team can then direct your enquiry to the appropriate support channel.

02 Can brands partner with VYBE?

Yes. Brands and organisations can reach out regarding event partnerships, collaborations, campaigns and live experiences.

03 How can artists connect with VYBE?

Artists and creators can use the artist and creator enquiry route to introduce themselves, share their work and discuss future opportunities.

04 Can I contact VYBE about hosting an event?

You can send your event idea through the contact form. Include the event type, location, expected audience and any important details.

05 What should I include in my enquiry?

A clear subject, your contact information and a short explanation of what you need will help us understand your request quickly.
